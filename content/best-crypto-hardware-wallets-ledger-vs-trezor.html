2. Trezor Safe 3 — Best for Open-Source Purists

For users who prefer completely open-source firmware, the Trezor Safe 3 is a phenomenal option. It introduces a dedicated secure element chip to protect against physical pin-extraction attacks while maintaining transparent open-source code.

Golden Rules of Hardware Wallet Security

  • Never enter your seed phrase on a computer or phone keyboard. Only type it into the physical hardware wallet device itself.
  • Never photograph or save your recovery phrase in cloud notes. Use physical paper or an engraved metal seed plate.
  • Verify every recipient address directly on your hardware wallet screen before confirming any transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Software wallets like MetaMask keep your private keys on an internet-connected device, exposing them to malware, browser exploits, and clipboard drainers. A hardware wallet stores your private keys inside an isolated secure element chip that never touches the internet.

Ledger Nano X features a certified EAL6+ Secure Element chip and Bluetooth connectivity for iOS/Android smartphones. Trezor Safe 3 is open-source hardware with an EAL6+ chip, optimized for desktop use without battery or Bluetooth.

Your crypto is not stored on the physical device; it lives on the blockchain. As long as you have your 12 or 24-word recovery seed phrase, you can restore your entire wallet on any new hardware or software wallet in minutes.

Tangem uses NFC cards with an EAL6+ chip where keys are generated directly on the card and backed up across 2-3 duplicate physical cards. This eliminates seed phrase theft, though recovery requires access to one of your physical backup cards.
