Important Crypto Tax Saving Tips (2024)

  1. Harvest Tax Losses: Sell losing positions before the tax year ends to offset capital gains and up to $3,000 of ordinary income in the US.
  2. Hold Past 12 Months: Long-term capital gains rates (0%–20%) are drastically lower than short-term ordinary income rates (up to 37%).
  3. Track Your Gas: Every dollar spent on Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Solana gas is a potential tax deduction against trading proceeds.
