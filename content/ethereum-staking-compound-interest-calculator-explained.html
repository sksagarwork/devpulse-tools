Ethereum Staking APY: How Compound Interest Works (+ Free Calculator)

By CryptoCalc Pro • Updated October 2024 • 8 min read

Ethereum staking is one of the most popular ways to earn passive income in crypto. But understanding the difference between APR and APY, and how compounding frequency affects your final returns, can mean thousands of dollars over time. This guide explains every detail — and our free calculator lets you model any scenario instantly.

APR vs APY: The Critical Difference

APR (Annual Percentage Rate) is the simple interest rate without compounding. APY (Annual Percentage Yield) accounts for the frequency at which your rewards are reinvested.

APY = (1 + APR/n)^n − 1

where n = compounding periods per year

Native ETH Staking vs Liquid Staking

Native staking requires 32 ETH and a validator node. Rewards are distributed per epoch (~6.4 minutes) but not automatically re-staked unless you run additional tooling. Liquid staking protocols (Lido, Rocket Pool, Frax ETH) handle compounding automatically and require no minimum.

Worked Example: $10,000 at 4.2% APY for 3 Years (Daily Compounding)

  • Year 1: \$10,000 → \$10,429
  • Year 2: \$10,429 → \$10,876
  • Year 3: \$10,876 → \$11,341
  • Total gain: \$1,341 (13.4%)

Tax Considerations for Staking Rewards

In the US, the IRS treats staking rewards as ordinary income at the time of receipt. This means each reward distribution is a taxable event at its fair market value — even before you sell. Track your rewards daily if possible, or use a crypto tax tool like Koinly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Native ETH staking on the Beacon Chain currently yields approximately 3.5%–4.5% APY depending on validator participation rates. Liquid staking protocols like Lido (stETH) offer similar rates with daily auto-compounding.

With compound interest, your staking rewards are reinvested to earn additional rewards. For example, daily compounding at 5% APR produces a final APY of approximately 5.13% — meaningfully higher than simple interest over a full year.

APY (Annual Percentage Yield) accounts for compounding and is always higher than or equal to APR for the same rate. When comparing protocols, always use APY for a fair comparison.

stETH from Lido rebases daily — your token balance increases automatically. rETH from Rocket Pool appreciates in price relative to ETH instead. Both compound daily, but in different ways.

Use the Rule of 72: divide 72 by your APY. At 4% APY, your investment doubles in approximately 18 years of compounding. At 8% APY (common for DeFi), it doubles in ~9 years.
