How to Calculate Crypto Profit & ROI (Including Exchange Fees)

By CryptoCalc Pro • Updated October 2024 • 8 min read

Most crypto traders focus on the buy and sell price but forget that exchange fees quietly erode profits. A trade showing 5% gain can easily become 4.6% after fees. This guide teaches you to calculate true profit and ROI accurately — accounting for every cost.

The Complete Profit Formula

Gross Profit   = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity
Entry Fee      = Buy Price × Quantity × Fee Rate
Exit Fee       = Sell Price × Quantity × Fee Rate
Net Profit     = Gross Profit − Entry Fee − Exit Fee
ROI%           = Net Profit / (Buy Price × Quantity) × 100

Example: Buying 0.5 BTC at $40,000, Selling at $46,000 (Binance 0.1% fee)

ItemAmount
Gross Profit(\$46,000 − \$40,000) × 0.5 = \$3,000
Entry Fee\$40,000 × 0.5 × 0.001 = \$20
Exit Fee\$46,000 × 0.5 × 0.001 = \$23
Net Profit\$3,000 − \$20 − \$23 = \$2,957
ROI%\$2,957 / \$20,000 = 14.79%

Break-Even Price After Fees

Before placing a trade, know your break-even: the minimum sell price to recover your full investment including both buy and sell fees.

Break-even = Buy Price × (1 + fee%) / (1 − fee%)
Example: \$40,000 × 1.001 / 0.999 = \$40,080.08
