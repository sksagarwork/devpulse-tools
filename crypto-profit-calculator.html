❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Net Profit = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity − Entry Fee − Exit Fee. Always subtract both fees from gross profit to get true net profit.
ROI% = (Net Profit / Amount Invested) × 100. A 50% ROI on a $1,000 investment means $500 net profit.
Binance charges 0.1% per trade (0.075% with BNB). A $10,000 round-trip trade incurs $20 in fees — significant for small gains but modest for long-term holds.
Break-even = Buy Price × (1 + fee_rate) / (1 − fee_rate). At 0.1% fee, a $40,000 buy breaks even at $40,080.
