How Crypto Capital Gains Tax Works

When you dispose of cryptocurrency (selling for fiat currency, swapping token A for token B, or buying goods), you trigger a taxable disposal event. If held for greater than 12 months, many jurisdictions grant reduced long-term capital gains tax rates (0%, 15%, or 20% in the United States).
