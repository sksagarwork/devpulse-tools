Why Dollar-Cost Averaging Beats Market Timing

Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) is the most proven risk-mitigation strategy in volatile crypto markets. By purchasing fixed dollar amounts at regular intervals, investors automatically buy more units when prices dip and fewer when prices rise, eliminating emotional trading traps.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

DCA means investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price. It reduces the impact of volatility and removes the need to time the market.
Lump sum outperforms DCA ~65% of the time in bull markets. DCA significantly outperforms in ranging or declining markets and reduces maximum drawdown.
Weekly DCA generally outperforms monthly in volatile markets as it captures more price variation. Daily DCA captures even more but fees can erode returns for small amounts.
Average cost = Total invested / Total units acquired. Total units = sum of (Investment per period / Price per period) across all intervals.
