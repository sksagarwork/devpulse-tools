How Ethereum Gas Fees Work (EIP-1559 Explained)

Since the London Hard Fork (EIP-1559), every Ethereum transaction fee consists of two elements: the Base Fee (which is automatically burned by the protocol, removing ETH from circulation) and the Priority Fee (Tip) given directly to validators to incentivize faster inclusion.

Total Fee (ETH) = Gas Limit × (Base Fee + Priority Fee) in Gwei × 10^-9
Total Fee (USD) = Total Fee (ETH) × ETH Price in USD

Network congestion directly dictates the base fee. When network demand exceeds the target 15 million gas per block, the base fee increases automatically by up to 12.5% per block.
