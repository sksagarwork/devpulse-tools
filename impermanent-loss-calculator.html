The Mathematics Behind Impermanent Loss

In standard automated market makers like Uniswap V2 and SushiSwap, liquidity pools maintain an invariant product: x * y = k. When the price ratio between the two pooled assets shifts by a factor of k_ratio, the percentage of impermanent loss is calculated using the formula:

IL = (2 * sqrt(PriceRatio) / (1 + PriceRatio)) - 1

If fee revenue generated by pool swaps exceeds the calculated impermanent loss, your position remains net profitable compared to holding.
