High-Intent Questions Answered

What is Impermanent Loss in AMM Liquidity Pools? ▼
Impermanent loss occurs when the price ratio of pooled assets diverges from when you deposited them. The greater the divergence, the more value you lose compared to simply holding the tokens in your wallet.
How is Crypto Tax Calculated? ▼
In most jurisdictions (such as the US, UK, EU, and Australia), crypto is treated as property. Selling, trading one token for another, or spending crypto triggers a taxable capital gain or loss based on your cost basis.
