How Crypto Futures Liquidation is Calculated

In cryptocurrency derivative exchanges (like Binance, Bybit, and OKX), your position is liquidated when your margin balance falls below the required maintenance margin. For Long positions: Liq Price = Entry * (1 - (1/Leverage) + MMR).
