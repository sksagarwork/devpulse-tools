APR vs. APY: The Power of Compounding in Staking

APR represents simple interest without compounding. When staking rewards are restaked (auto-compounding daily or weekly), your effective yield increases to APY: APY = (1 + r/n)^n - 1. Over multi-year horizons, frequent compounding creates exponential portfolio growth.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Native ETH staking currently yields approximately 3.5%–4.5% APY. Liquid staking protocols like Lido offer similar rates with automatic daily compounding.
APR is the simple interest rate before compounding. APY accounts for reinvestment of rewards. At 5% APR with daily compounding, APY is ~5.13%.
More frequent compounding slightly increases final returns. Daily compounding at 5% APR yields 5.127% APY vs 5.0% for annual compounding.
In the US, staking rewards are ordinary income at receipt. In the UK and Australia, tax treatment varies — consult a crypto tax advisor for your jurisdiction.
