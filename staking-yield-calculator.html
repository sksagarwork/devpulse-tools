APR vs. APY: The Power of Compounding in Staking

APR represents simple interest without compounding. When staking rewards are restaked (auto-compounding daily or weekly), your effective yield increases to APY: APY = (1 + r/n)^n - 1. Over multi-year horizons, frequent compounding creates exponential portfolio growth.
